PRIVACY POLICY — RavenSDR
Last updated: June 2026

RavenSDR is a Software Defined Radio (SDR) receiver application for Android.
This policy describes how the app handles your information.

DATA COLLECTION
RavenSDR does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.
The app has no internet permission and cannot send data of any kind to
any server, third party, or developer.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
The app saves the following information locally on your device only:
  - Playlist entries you create (frequency name, frequency, modulation type)
  - Alert sensitivity setting

This data is stored using Android's standard local storage (SharedPreferences)
and never leaves your device.

HARDWARE ACCESS
The app accesses a USB-connected RTL-SDR hardware dongle to receive radio
signals. No audio is recorded or transmitted. All received radio signals are
processed locally in real time and are not saved or shared.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
RavenSDR uses no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or
tracking services.

THIRD-PARTY LIBRARIES
The app uses the following open-source libraries, none of which collect data:
  - AndroidX AppCompat (Google) — UI compatibility
  - Material Components (Google) — UI components
  - librtlsdr — RTL-SDR hardware driver
  - libusb — USB communication
  - KissFFT — Signal processing

CHILDREN
The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new date.
